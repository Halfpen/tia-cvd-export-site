Privacy policy

Tia CVD Export is a personal utility operated by Halfpen. Last updated: 5 October 2026.

Purpose and data

The application exports cryptocurrency market CVD records from the operator’s local database to a designated Google Drive folder. It creates or updates a CSV and a status file, and reads their metadata and checksums to verify uploads.

Google account access

The operator authorizes Google Drive access using OAuth. Credentials and refresh tokens are stored on the operator’s devices for scheduled uploads. The configured Drive scope may permit broader file access than this export workflow uses; the exporter is configured to use its designated export folder.

Use and sharing

The exporter uses Google data only to perform and verify the requested file synchronization. It does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. Exported market files may be accessed by analysis tools separately authorized by the operator. Google processes files and authorization information as the Drive service provider.

Storage and deletion

Exported files remain in the operator’s Google Drive until replaced or deleted. Local records, configuration and backups remain on the operator’s devices until removed. The operator can stop scheduled uploads, revoke this application’s access in Google Account permissions, delete the exported Drive files, and remove local credentials and backups. Revoking access does not itself delete previously exported files.

This website

These static information pages contain no application analytics or advertising scripts. GitHub Pages provides hosting and may process request information under its own privacy policy.
